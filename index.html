HOW TO PLAY

PC Controls

A / D or ← / → — Move between covers

Mouse — Aim   Left Click — Shoot (hold to peek)

R — Reload   1 2 3 / Scroll — Switch weapon

Mobile Controls

Use on-screen buttons for movement, firing, reloading, and weapon switching.

Objective

Eliminate all enemies from behind cover. Peek to shoot but watch your health! Complete mission objectives for up to 3 stars.

Tips

  • Covers can be destroyed — relocate when needed
  • Shoot incoming missiles to destroy them
  • Snipers show a red laser before firing — take cover!
  • Bosses are vulnerable when launching missiles
